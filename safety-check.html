Safety Guidelines

Our community is built on trust. Review our comprehensive guides for digital and physical safety.

Digital Safety First

Keep your private data off the chat. Use LUNARA's secure messaging for all coordination.

Meetup Etiquette

Respect boundaries and always inform a trusted friend about your plans.

LUNARA provides complete transparency and smart payment controls to protect you from unfair charges.
  • View complete venue menus and pricing before you book.
  • Use Split Payment to pay only for your share.
  • Verify all applicable charges (GST, Service Fees) in advance.

Interacting safely in the digital space is vital. Keep your personal data secure.
  • Keep conversations within LUNARA until you fully trust the person.
  • Watch for users who push for inappropriate video calls.
  • Use our Block & Report tools for any discomfort.
